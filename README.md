# LLM Training for Product Professionals

This repository contains the materials and anonymized survey data used in a study investigating the impact of technical training on Large Language Models (LLMs) for Product professionals.

The training was designed to introduce computational foundations of AI and LLMs to professionals involved in product decision-making in industrial environments.

## Repository Contents

### Slides

Slides used during the course:

- **Treinamento LLM - Parte 1.pptx** – Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models
- **Treinamento LLM - Parte 2.pptx** – Architectural implications, risks, and decision-making considerations for LLM-based products

The course covers topics such as:

- Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence
- How Large Language Models work
- Limitations of probabilistic AI systems
- Architectural implications of LLMs in digital products
- Risks and governance in AI-based systems

### Dataset

`Respostas Questionários Pré e Pós Curso.xlsx`

The dataset contains anonymized responses from participants collected before and after the training.

The questionnaire includes:

- Likert-scale questions about perceived understanding of AI and LLM concepts
- Questions about the perceived ability to apply these concepts in product development decisions
- Awareness of risks associated with AI systems
- One optional open-ended question

All responses were anonymized before publication.

## Study Context

The training was delivered to Product professionals working in an industrial technology environment, including roles such as:

- Product Owners
- Product Managers
- Product Designers
- Product Leads

The goal of the intervention was to evaluate whether short technical training on LLM fundamentals could improve participants' perceived understanding and decision-making capacity regarding AI-based products.
